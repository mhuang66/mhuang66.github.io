5-Minute Breakfasts

A 5-minute breakfast is a quick and easy way to start your day on a nutritious note. It ensures you get a healthy meal even on the busiest mornings. With simple ingredients and minimal prep, you can enjoy a satisfying breakfast in no time.

These fast breakfast ideas are perfect for busy schedules and help you avoid the temptation of grabbing less healthy options on the go. By incorporating a variety of nutrient-rich ingredients, you can easily adapt these recipes to fit your dietary preferences and goals. Enjoy a range of flavors and textures while keeping your mornings stress-free and nutritious.

Avocado Toast with Egg

Ingredients

  • 1 slice whole-grain bread
  • ½ avocado
  • 1 egg
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread
  2. Mash the avocado in a small bowl
  3. Fry or scramble the egg in a non-stick pan
  4. Spread avocado on the toast
  5. Top with the egg
  6. Season with salt and pepper

Cottage Cheese and Fruit Bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • ½ cup mixed fruit
  • 1 tbsp honey.

Instructions

  1. Spoon the cottage cheese into a bowl
  2. Top with mixed fruit
  3. Drizzle with honey

Microwave Scrambled Eggs

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • salt and pepper
  • salt and pepper
  • optional toppings like cheese or herbs

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs and milk
  2. Season with salt and pepper
  3. Microwave on high for about 1 minute, stirring halfway through
  4. Spread avocado on the toast
  5. Top with cheese or herbs if desired

Overnight Oats

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • ½ cup fresh fruit
  • 1 tsp honey

Instructions

  1. Mix oats, milk, and chia seeds in a jar
  2. Close jar and leave overnight
  3. Stir and top with fresh fruit and honey

Banana Oatmeal Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 egg
  • a pinch of cinnamon.

Instructions

  1. Blend the banana, oats, egg, and cinnamon until smooth
  2. Pour small amounts of the batter into a hot, non-stick pan to form pancakes
  3. Fry or scramble the egg in a non-stick pan
  4. Cook for about 1 minute on each side, until golden brown
  5. Serve with your choice of toppings

Berry Yogurt Parfait

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup mixed berries
  • ¼ cup granola
  • 1 tbsp honey

Instructions

  1. In a bowl or jar, layer the Greek yogurt, berries, and granola
  2. Drizzle with honey

Peanut Butter Banana Smoothie

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 cup almond milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients until smooth
  2. Pour into a glass

Spinach and Feta Omelette Wrap

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • a handful of spinach
  • 2 tbsp crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 wheat tortilla

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs and cook them in a non-stick pan with thje spinach until the eggs are set
  2. Sprinkle feta cheese on top
  3. Wrap it all in a tortilla
